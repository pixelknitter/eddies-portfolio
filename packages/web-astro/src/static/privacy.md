I'm Eddie Freeman. This is my personal site. This page explains what happens to
your information when you use it, in plain language, because a policy you can't
read isn't much of a promise.

## The short version

- **No accounts.** There's nothing to sign up for.
- **No advertising, no ad networks, no cross-site tracking.** Nothing here follows
  you anywhere else.
- **Nothing is sold or shared for marketing.** Not now, not later.
- **No session recording.** I don't replay your visit or watch your mouse move.
- **No database of visitors.** When you fill in a form, the details reach me and
  are not saved into a database here.

If you only read this far, that's the substance of it.

## What you send me on purpose

Two forms on this site ask for something: the request for access to A.I.R., and
the request for a copy of my resume. Both ask for **your email address** and **a
short note about why you're asking**. The resume form also asks which format you
want.

When you submit one:

- The details are sent to a private channel I read, so I can respond.
- If you're asking for resume PDFs, links come straight back to your browser.
- If you're asking for A.I.R. access and I approve it, you get an email with an
  access code.

**They aren't written to a database.** The site has no visitor table, no CRM, no
mailing list. Your note lives in the message I received and in the signed link
that carries it, and nothing else.

One detail worth knowing: an A.I.R. access code issued to you **has your email
address encoded inside it**. That's deliberate — it means a code that gets passed
around can be traced back to who it was issued to. It also means the code is
personal to you and worth treating like a password.

I'll use your address to reply to you about what you asked for. I won't add you to
a newsletter, because there isn't one.

## If you ask A.I.R. a question

A.I.R. answers questions about my work using things I've written, and it's
currently available by invitation.

- **Your question is sent to Anthropic**, whose model generates the answer. It is
  covered by their terms and privacy practices, not just mine.
- **Your question and the answer are recorded in my analytics for 30 days**, then
  automatically deleted. I read them to find out where A.I.R. gives a poor answer
  or has nothing useful to say — that's how it gets better.
- **They are not linked to your email address or your identity.** The record shows
  what was asked and what came back, not who asked it.
- If you rate an answer, that rating is recorded against the answer. If you add a
  comment, please don't put anything sensitive in it — it's a free-text box and I
  read what arrives.

Don't send A.I.R. anything confidential. It's a public-facing feature backed by a
third-party model, and it isn't the right place for private information.

## The resume PDFs have your email in them

If I send you a resume PDF, **your email address is written into the file itself**
as a small line of text.

I'm telling you rather than hiding it. The reason is simple: my full resume has
contact details that aren't published on this site, and the watermark means a copy
that gets forwarded can be traced back to the request it came from. It's
attribution, not restriction. You can read it, print it, and share it with your
team — I'd just rather it not end up on a scraper site with no idea where it came
from.

## What's collected automatically

**Analytics.** I use PostHog to see whether the site works — which pages load,
where something breaks, and whether A.I.R. is answering well. It's configured
narrowly on purpose:

- Events are **anonymous**. I don't create a profile for you and I don't attach
  your email address to anything.
- **No session replay**, no heatmaps, no scroll tracking.
- Automatic click-and-form capture is **switched off**, so the contents of form
  fields and download links are never collected.
- One cookie is set, to tell one visit apart from another.

**Server logs.** Cloudflare hosts this site, so it necessarily handles your IP
address to deliver a page to you, and keeps short-lived request logs. That's
ordinary hosting, not something extra I've added.

**Rate limiting.** The request forms allow a handful of submissions per visitor in
a short window, so nobody can hammer them. That check uses your IP address, holds
it in memory for a few minutes, and never writes it down.

**Your theme preference.** If you switch between light and dark, that choice is
saved in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device and it isn't a
cookie.

## Who else touches your information

Four services, each doing one job:

| Service | What it's for | What it sees |
|---|---|---|
| **Cloudflare** | Hosting, email delivery | Your IP address and requests; the emails I send you |
| **Anthropic** | Generates A.I.R. answers | The question you asked |
| **PostHog** | Analytics | Anonymous usage events; A.I.R. questions and answers for 30 days |
| **Discord** | Where I read incoming requests | Your email address and your note |

Each has its own privacy policy. I've kept the list this short deliberately —
every additional service is another place your information sits.

## How long things are kept

| Thing | How long |
|---|---|
| Your email and note | In the message I received, until I delete it. Not stored in a database. |
| A resume download link | 15 minutes, then it stops working |
| An A.I.R. approval link | 7 days, then it stops working |
| A.I.R. questions and answers | 30 days, then deleted automatically |
| Anonymous usage counts | Kept, with no personal information in them |
| Server logs | A short period, set by Cloudflare |

## What I don't do

- Sell, rent, or trade your information.
- Run advertising or share data with ad networks.
- Track you across other websites.
- Record your session or your keystrokes.
- Add you to a mailing list because you asked for a PDF.
- Use your question to train a model of my own. I don't train models.

## Your choices

**Don't want to be counted?** Analytics here are anonymous, but if you'd rather
not appear at all, a tracker blocker will stop them and the site works perfectly
well without them. Nothing on this site requires analytics to function.

**Want your details removed?** Email me at
[privacy@eddie.engineering](mailto:privacy@eddie.engineering) and I'll delete
what I have and tell you what it was. Since almost nothing is stored, this is
usually a short conversation.

**Want a copy of what I hold?** Same address. Same answer, usually.

If you're in the UK or EU, you have rights over your personal data under the GDPR
— including access, correction, deletion, and objection. I'm one person running a
small site, so the honest route to exercising any of them is to email me and ask.

## Children

This site is aimed at people interested in my professional work. It isn't
directed at children, and I don't knowingly collect information from anyone under
16.
